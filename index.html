Selected work

Key projects

Four projects, from robot arms that learn from one demonstration to hospital data at scale.

01IEEE RAAICON 2026

One demonstration, fifty robot skills

A webcam hand-tracking interface teaches a simulated Franka Panda arm in MuJoCo. One recorded demo becomes 50 physics-validated training episodes.

98%behaviour-cloning success with geometry-preserving augmentation, vs 48% with naive noise
Paper ↗
02IEEE RAAICON 2026

Safe warehouse navigation learned from human driving

A TurtleBot3 learns local navigation from teleoperation in a Webots warehouse (ROS 2 Jazzy), wrapped in a safety layer that reasons about gaps between moving obstacles.

65→2.4%time spent dangerously close to obstacles
Paper ↗
03arXiv 2025

AI-driven flood monitoring

Pixel-wise water segmentation for emergency response, trained on a new dataset that combines benchmark, drone, in-field and social-media flood images.

90.57%validation accuracy, DeepLab v3 (vs U-Net 87.12%)
arXiv ↗
04B.Sc. thesis · arXiv 2025

Predicting how long patients stay in hospital

A Spark pipeline on AWS over New York inpatient records, finding what drives length of stay and predicting it at admission with tree ensembles, process mining and simulation.

2.3Mde-identified inpatient records analysed

How I work

Principles

Rigour

Report what the data supports, including the negative results. Five seeds, paired tests, and no claims a comparison can't back up.

People first
